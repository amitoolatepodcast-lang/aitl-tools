Do you know the true cost of your lifestyle?

Most of us underestimate what we spend — until the bills pile up. That's why I built the Lifestyle Calculator.

  • Turn big annual or irregular bills into simple monthly costs — so nothing sneaks up on you.
  • See where your money really goes — not just rent and groceries, but the extras that shape your lifestyle.
  • Understand your balance of Needs, Wants and Future — and check whether it matches the life you actually want.
  • Get a clearer picture of your retirement target — so you can plan for security and freedom.
